## Budget chart

The proportional budget chart is drawn for this lesson using [USGS Circular 1544, table 1, printed page 8](../sources/usgs-earthquake-strategy-2024-2033.pdf#page=20). The FY2024 Earthquake Hazards Program appropriation totals $92.651 million: $62.645 million monitoring and reporting, $16.376 million assessment, and $13.630 million research.
