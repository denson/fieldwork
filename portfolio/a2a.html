    03 / Check your understanding

    Eight decisions to get right.

    Choose an answer for each question, then review the explanations. The practice threshold is 6 of 8, including all three critical questions about authority, recurring terms and uncertain payments. This is a learning exercise, not professional certification.

    Try an advanced explanation

    An agent has a $50 one-time allowance. A seller offers $10/month. The payment request times out, then the A2A task reports completion. Explain what must be checked before anyone calls the order paid or accepted.
