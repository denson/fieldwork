# Business Plan First Steps

An independent Fieldwork / Stoagen learning activity for someone making a first business-plan draft. Approximately 15–20 minutes; blank answers are allowed and remain marked. A learning draft is not a complete financing application.

- Website: https://denson.github.io/fieldwork/?demo=business
- Matching companion: https://box.boodle.ai/a/@BusinessPlanFirstSteps
- Feedback: https://denson.github.io/fieldwork/?demo=business&step=review
- Portfolio: https://denson.github.io/fieldwork/?demo=home

## Six steps

1. **idea:** name and describe the business in an ordinary sentence.
2. **customer:** choose a first customer group, explain its problem and separate evidence from assumptions.
3. **offer:** define one purchase, alternatives, first marketing channel, delivery and resources or requirements to check.
4. **numbers:** define a sale; enter unit price, variable cost per sale, monthly fixed costs and expected monthly sales. Capacity and one-time startup spending are optional.
5. **test:** choose a small real-world test, a useful signal, a next action and a question for an adviser.
6. **review:** review, copy, download or print the first draft; separately prepare feedback.

Step links use only `?demo=business&step=idea` (or customer, offer, numbers, test, review). No freeform answers are put in URLs. Answers remain in this browser tab where session storage works, and are not uploaded by the website. A copied link does not contain the user's draft. Download the draft before closing the tab.

## Fictional worked example

Mesa Yard Care is a made-up one-person service considering two-hour yard-care visits for older homeowners in one Pueblo neighborhood. It excludes tree work and pesticide application. Demand has not been researched. Before a paid visit the owner needs to check equipment, transport, insurance, local requirements and safe scope of work.

At $90 per visit, $20 variable cost per visit, $700 monthly fixed costs and 20 visits, revenue is $1,800, variable costs are $400, and the operating remainder is $700. Each sale contributes $70 toward fixed costs. Ten visits cover those fixed costs. With 25% fewer visits, rounded down to 15, the remainder is $350. Example capacity is 30 visits and startup spending is $1,800.

These are fictional teaching inputs, not local price or demand evidence. The operating remainder is before owner pay unless entered in costs, income taxes, debt payments and recovery of startup spending. Startup costs are shown separately. This is not a cash-flow forecast and does not model when payments arrive.

## Model rules

Revenue = price × monthly sales. Variable costs = cost per sale × sales. Operating remainder = revenue − variable costs − monthly fixed costs. Contribution per sale = price − cost per sale. Break-even whole sales = ceiling(monthly fixed costs ÷ positive contribution per sale). No positive contribution means no attainable break-even in this model. Blank is not zero. Sales and capacity are non-negative whole numbers. Capacity warnings compare expected sales and break-even with entered capacity.

## Guide handoff

Each step prepares a short visible note. The learner deliberately places it in the matching BoodleBox draft using the extension, or copies and pastes it, then presses Send. The bot cannot see unshared fields or later clicks. A returned link navigates a step; it does not fill answers or demonstrate completion. The extension needs version 0.7.0 for this new companion mapping.

## Reviewer feedback

The reviewer can record a name (optional), whether they would use this with a new business owner, what helped, where they got confused, and what they would change. The exact package is visible. Business-plan answers are excluded unless the reviewer checks Include my business-plan draft.

Download feedback as a text file, copy it, or open an email draft. Short feedback fits in the email body. Long feedback uses a short email with instructions to manually attach the downloaded file. No attachment is added automatically. The reviewer supplies the recipient email address if one is not configured. Only the reviewer sends the email. The website and bot cannot confirm delivery or read that email. There is no email service, server storage or telemetry.
