# Hidden-seafloor investigation — 27 September 2026

The previous constant-depth crossing exercise is retired. Its [test record](TSUNAMI-SLIDER-TEST-2026-09-27.md) preserves the failure in which a bot confused modeled crossing time with warning lead time.

## Built and checked locally

- Three fictional starter soundings at 0, 50 and 100 km produce an estimated deep seafloor. The actual profile is withheld until the learner predicts.
- In a narrow mobile browser, I measured at 25, 55, 65 and 85 km. The prediction and reveal stayed unavailable until all four soundings were taken. I predicted a hidden ridge and then revealed the profile. The map distinguished measured points, the dashed estimate and the revealed profile. The reviewed note contained all four depths, including the 800 m reading at 65 km, and the prediction.
- The bounded state tests cover a sounding that changes the estimated map, duplicates and budget limits, prediction before reveal, export wording and restoration of older saved lesson answers. The full portfolio test suite passed.
- The published Earthquake & Tsunami Guide instructions now describe the new activity and retain explicit real-warning boundaries. Its attached lesson was replaced with the current file.
