Earthquake data

The Earthquake Explorer calls the USGS GeoJSON summary feeds directly from the visitor’s browser. Records and magnitudes may be revised. The display preserves feed-generation time, and exported notes identify their snapshot and event sources.

Dot experiment

Patterns and measurements are produced by this website. The experiment is illustrative: browser timing, practice, and the small number of rounds limit interpretation. No comparison to a normative population is made.

Original illustrations

The casebook and counting-counter artwork were created for this portfolio with an AI image-generation tool. They are labeled as illustrations. They do not depict historical events, and they are separate from the authentic Library of Congress photographs and the scored dot patterns.

About this portfolio

Fieldwork is an independent portfolio by Denson Smith. It is not an official BoodleBox, Library of Congress, NOAA, or USGS product.

The site has no account system, analytics, result database, or private API key. Notes and game results stay in the open tab until copied or downloaded. The earthquake activity contacts USGS when loading or refreshing a feed. External source links open the original providers.
