# BoodleBox bot evaluation ledger

Updated 27 September 2026. This records observed behavior, not a ranking of model vendors. A published bot is not necessarily a passed bot. Most checks below used the author's signed-in account; a separate recipient test is still needed where noted. Add a dated row and a chat or test-record link after each substantive test. Do not turn a promising first answer into a production claim.

**Output modes:** Bot-only, workspace-only, and bot-plus-workspace experiences are all valid. Evaluate each mode against its own task: a paired mode needs its workspace; a workspace-only mode needs no BoodleBox bot; a chat-only claim needs an end-to-end chat test. The same bot may support both chat-only and paired modes if both actually pass. A partial draft is not a complete-mode pass. For an AI-ready workspace, record the discoverable entry and readable reference material separately from any tested ability to operate its interactive controls.

**Access & transparency:** Record two independent axes for each output: who may use the BoodleBox bot, and whether its instructions/knowledge are intentionally published; then who may access the workspace, its AI-readable files, and any MCP/API services. Use restricted, shared interface with unpublished materials, or open interface and source as draft levels on each axis. BoodleBox enforces its bot/chat access; the website host and connected services enforce workspace access. A shared bot still requires a BoodleBox account, and a public page cannot conceal material delivered in its HTML or JavaScript. Unknown permissions, remix settings and backend protections remain **unverified**, not private by assumption. See BoodleBox’s [bot sharing](https://help.boodlebox.ai/article/share-bots), [chat permissions](https://help.boodlebox.ai/article/share-chat), and [remix behavior](https://help.boodlebox.ai/article/remix-bots).

## How to use this ledger

For each new or revised bot, record the task, model shown in BoodleBox, exact input, observed output, working source links, failure or uncertainty, chat URL, and decision. Keep **author-account functional pass**, **separate-recipient pass**, and **production-ready** distinct. Any invented action, wrong citation, unsupported device-specific step, or false claim of checking a source is a fail for a real-world support task even when the rest of the reply sounds helpful.

The two device-support BoodleBox profiles were republished on 27 September with retired names, warnings in their greetings and descriptions, and instructions not to provide troubleshooting. Their aliases remain only so old links show the retirement notice. The former companion page likewise opens with a retirement notice and leaves old locally saved cases available for export. No device-support bot appears in the public Fieldwork example menu.
