## How to use this ledger

For each new or revised bot, record the task, model shown in BoodleBox, exact input, observed output, working source links, failure or uncertainty, chat URL, and decision. Keep **author-account functional pass**, **separate-recipient pass**, and **production-ready** distinct. Any invented action, wrong citation, unsupported device-specific step, or false claim of checking a source is a fail for a real-world support task even when the rest of the reply sounds helpful.

The two device-support BoodleBox profiles were republished on 27 September with retired names, warnings in their greetings and descriptions, and instructions not to provide troubleshooting. Their aliases remain only so old links show the retirement notice. The former companion page likewise opens with a retirement notice and leaves old locally saved cases available for export. No device-support bot appears in the public Fieldwork example menu.
