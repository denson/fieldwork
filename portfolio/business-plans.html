FROM EXAMPLE TO ADVISER CONVERSATION

Turn an example into your first draft.

  1. Choose and change.

    Read a filled-in example. Change the customer or offer to something you understand. Replace teaching numbers with your estimates when ready.

  2. Find the questions that matter.

    Try the calculator and open See my brief. Record customer evidence, delivery limits, cost quotes, cash needs and launch checks.

  3. Leave with a next step.

    Download or print the draft with its gaps. Choose one question to investigate, who will do it and when. Bring the draft to your adviser when you are ready.

Where the examples come from

We Can Do It Consulting adapts Rebecca’s SBA example. Wooden Grain Toy Company draws on Andrew’s lean example and traditional example. They are two businesses, with the lean and detailed approaches combined here.

Mesa Yard Care, Porchside Bike Tune-Ups and SafeStart Property Testing are original Fieldwork examples organized around SBA planning topics. Fieldwork adds original scope, operating details, illustrative financial inputs and review questions to all five.

All calculator numbers are fictional teaching assumptions, not current prices, demand evidence or SBA forecasts. Historical market and regulatory claims from the source plans are not carried forward. These are independent planning exercises, without SBA endorsement. The result is an initial draft for discussion; further research and forecasts may be needed for financing or launch.
