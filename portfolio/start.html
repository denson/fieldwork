What happens to my choices?

This practice saves your badge, topic, and checks in this browser when browser storage is available. Your return link includes the badge, topic, and a practice label, so it can display your pass in another browser too. Anyone with that link can see those choices. There is no name, password, or private answer in the link.

With the extension, Put note in BoodleBox transfers just the displayed note into the paired chat’s message box. It does not press Send or replace an existing draft. Without the extension, use copy and paste. Your later clicks and answers stay on the website until you share another note.
